## Interpretation safeguards

**Dedicated AI back-end fabrics are conditional.** Inference, RAG, fine-tuning and distributed training do not have identical transport, security or operational requirements.

**Security in Cisco Cloud Control** refers to the security-management experience within Cisco Cloud Control. The site does not represent it as an unrelated strategic platform.

**Cisco Live Protect** is for eligible vulnerabilities on eligible supported devices. It is an interim control, not a replacement for permanent remediation and software maintenance.

**Emerging capabilities** such as agentic operations require release-, entitlement-, scope- and maturity-specific validation.

**Architecture diagrams** are conceptual unless specifically labeled as customer-validated implementation drawings.
