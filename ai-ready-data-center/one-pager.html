Core principleAI readiness is a governed lifecycle, not a one-time hardware purchase.
Four architecture domains

Enterprise front-end

Applications, APIs, shared services and management

Optional AI back-end

Workload-driven GPU fabric and congestion controls

Storage & data

Ingestion, access, checkpoints and recovery

Cross-cutting governance

Security, observability, resilience, automation, lifecycle and skills

Capability adoption model

Foundational — Secure management, segmentation, telemetry, lifecycle, recovery and ownership.

Conditional — Dedicated GPU fabric, RoCEv2, Multi-Site and advanced workload segmentation when justified.

Emerging — Agentic operations and AI-assisted remediation under governed evaluation.
